# Data, model and redistribution notice

## VQA-RAD

VQA-RAD is available from OSF (DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/89KPS). Its question–answer metadata are released under CC0. The images originate from MedPix; explicit reuse terms for every source image have not been established here. No VQA-RAD image is redistributed by this repository.

The image-disjoint split used by the analysis was distributed by the RadGrounder repository. Because the licence of that upstream split has not been verified, the original train/validation/test JSON files are not included. Provenance and derived audit identifiers are retained without claiming ownership of upstream content.

## SLAKE

The official SLAKE project page does not state a licence. The `BoKelvin/SLAKE` Hugging Face distribution, uploaded by the first author, declares CC BY 4.0. Source images include:

- Medical Segmentation Decathlon: CC BY-SA 4.0;
- NIH ChestX-ray14: attribution and source acknowledgement requirements;
- CHAOS: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

The most restrictive applicable third-party terms remain controlling. This repository therefore contains no SLAKE source image or original dataset archive and is intended for academic, non-commercial reproducibility use.

## Released derived material

The repository may include question/sample identifiers, derived eligibility and split manifests, perturbation mappings, model outputs, configurations, statistics, resource measurements and analysis code. These materials do not grant rights to obtain or redistribute any underlying image.

## Models

MedGemma and BiomedCLIP remain subject to their respective upstream access conditions and licences. Users must obtain model access directly and comply with those terms. No checkpoint is included.
